## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a software engineer with **four years of industry experience**, now pursuing my **Master's in Computer Science at Northeastern University (Khoury College)**, graduating December 2027.

I came into software from **Electronics & Communication Engineering**, then spent four years at **Accenture** in Bengaluru building and supporting **AWS-hosted enterprise retail applications**. The part of that job I enjoyed most was **troubleshooting**: following a problem through the application, the infrastructure, and the database until it finally made sense. That's what pulled me toward **distributed systems and infrastructure**, the layer where things fail in the most interesting ways.

I learn by building. When I want to understand something, I implement it, break it on purpose, and then go read the paper.

⚡ **Fun fact:** I find the broken version of a system more interesting than the working one. The working one doesn't tell you much.

## 🧭 What I'm Into

- **Distributed systems:** consensus, replication, and what "correct" even means when machines disagree
- **Platform & infrastructure:** the layer everyone depends on and nobody notices until it fails
- **Observability:** getting a system to explain itself
- **ML systems:** what it takes to serve and scale models, not just train them
- **Software supply chain security:** trusting code you didn't write

## ❓ Questions I Keep Coming Back To

- What does a system actually agree on when the network is lying to it?
- How do you know a distributed system is correct, not just that it passed the tests?
- How much can you learn about a machine just by watching it?
- Should compute care *when* it runs, not just *where*?
- How do you trust a package you installed five seconds ago?
- What should sit between an application and a model?
